## Graduation Picture

To display your graduation picture in the hero section:
1. Place your graduation photo in this folder
2. Rename it to `grad-pic.jpg` (or .png)
3. The image will automatically appear above your name in the hero section

If the image is not found, a graduation cap emoji (🎓) will be displayed as a fallback.
